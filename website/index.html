construction.

01

Single-tenant deployment

Each client installs into their own Salesforce org. Their data, region, and sharing model stay theirs.

Permission-set access

Admin, agent, consent manager, template manager, bulk send. Least privilege is the default.

API logs, webhook events, rule logs, message history, retention windows, and legal hold.
